How Restaurants Can Use ChatGPT for Social Media Posts in English and Spanish

Cómo usar ChatGPT para tus publicaciones en español e inglés

Guía práctica para publicar en Facebook, Instagram y Google en inglés y español, con prompts listos para copiar y pegar.

November 17, 2026 · Social media & AI

If you run a restaurant in Phoenix, Mesa, Tucson, or anywhere else in Arizona, guests look you up on Facebook, Instagram, and Google, and many are more comfortable in Spanish, English, or both. ChatGPT (or another AI chat) can draft those posts in both languages so you spend your time editing, not starting from a blank page. You stay in charge. The tool does not know today's special or your hours unless you tell it, so read every draft before it goes live.

Give it the facts first

A prompt like "write a post for my restaurant" usually sounds generic. Include your restaurant name and city, the dish or offer, days and hours, where the post will go, and that you need English and natural Spanish, not a word-for-word translation. Fill in the brackets, paste the prompt, then edit the result so it sounds like you.

Dale los datos de tu restaurante. Si no se los das, el texto suena genérico.

Do not paste private customer information: phone numbers, order details, addresses, or anything a guest would not expect to see in a public post. For a review reply, use only the public text of the review.

Five prompts you can copy and paste

Do not invent prices, ingredients, hours, offers, or dietary claims. If a detail is missing, ask instead of guessing.

1. This week's special

Pair it with a photo of the real plate. You can also turn the same special into a Google post; see 7 Ways to Fill Slow Weekdays at Your Arizona Restaurant.

English:

Write short Facebook and Instagram posts for [restaurant name] in [city, Arizona]. This week's special is [dish]: [what's included], available [days and hours]. Friendly owner tone. Add a one-line caption for a photo of the real plate.

Español:

Escribe una publicación corta para Facebook e Instagram de [nombre del restaurante] en [ciudad, Arizona]. Especial: [platillo], incluye [qué incluye], disponible [días y horario]. Español natural, como se habla en Arizona, no una traducción literal. Tono cercano.

2. Holiday hours

Wrong hours frustrate guests and should match Google. For Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's, or a day you close early, write the hours first, then draft the post and update your Google Business Profile.

English:

Write a short Facebook/Instagram post and a shorter Google post for [restaurant name] in [city, Arizona]. [Holiday]: we are [open/closed]. If open, hours are [start]–[end]. Regular hours resume [day]. English first, then natural Spanish.

Español:

Redacta una publicación corta y un texto más breve para el perfil de Google de [nombre del restaurante] en [ciudad, Arizona]. [Día festivo]: estamos [abiertos/cerrados]. Si abrimos: [hora]–[hora]. El horario normal vuelve el [día]. Español natural.

3. A new dish

English:

Announce a new menu item at [restaurant name] in [city, Arizona]: [name], [flavor and how it's served]. Available [starting when], for [dine-in and/or pickup]. Excited but honest. English, natural Spanish, and a short caption for a photo of the real dish.

Español:

Anuncia un platillo nuevo de [nombre del restaurante] en [ciudad, Arizona]: [nombre], [sabor y cómo se sirve]. Disponible [desde cuándo], para [comer en el local y/o recoger]. Tono entusiasta pero honesto. Español natural, no una traducción literal.

4. A thank-you for a review

Reply in the language of the review, mention one specific thing they liked, and do not argue or share private details.

English:

Draft a short thank-you reply to this public review of [restaurant name]: "[paste the review]". Mention one specific thing they liked. Warm and brief, like the owner. Do not offer anything in exchange for a review or ask them to change a rating. Same language as the review.

Español:

Redacta una respuesta breve de agradecimiento a esta reseña pública de [nombre del restaurante]: "[pega la reseña]". Menciona algo específico que les gustó. Tono cálido y corto. No ofrezcas nada a cambio ni pidas que cambien la calificación. Responde en el mismo idioma de la reseña.

5. A pickup reminder

If you have commission-free online ordering for pickup under your own brand, a midweek reminder helps when the dining room is quiet, especially in the heat or during a storm.

English:

Write a short Facebook, Instagram, and Google post for [restaurant name] in [city, Arizona]. Remind guests they can order ahead for pickup on our own ordering page: [link, or "the link is on our website and Google profile"]. Pickup hours: [hours]. Helpful, not pushy. English and natural Spanish.

Español:

Escribe una publicación corta recordando que en [nombre del restaurante] en [ciudad, Arizona] se puede pedir por adelantado para recoger en nuestra propia página: [enlace, o "el enlace está en nuestra web o en Google"]. Horario de recogida: [horario]. Tono útil, no agresivo. Español natural.

Revisa cada borrador antes de publicar. Tú conoces tu menú, el horario y a tus clientes.

Make the Spanish sound like your restaurant

Asking the chat to "translate this" often produces stiff, literal Spanish. Ask instead for natural Spanish, the way you would speak to a guest in Phoenix or Tucson. If Spanish is not your strongest language, have a bilingual teammate read the draft.

Use real photos, then add the caption

A clear phone photo of the real plate, taken near a window, does more for a post than polished copy alone. AI can help with the caption, and even brighten a photo, but the picture should show your food as it is served.

A simple weekly routine

  • Sunday: Choose the week's special or announcement and take one good photo.
  • Monday morning: Paste the matching prompt, edit both languages, and post on Facebook, Instagram, and your Google Business Profile.
  • Before holidays: Run the holiday-hours prompt and update your Google hours the same day.
  • When a kind review comes in: Draft the thank-you, check it, and reply in the reviewer's language.

Before you hit publish

Check hours, dates, and what's included. Confirm any price, ingredient, or dietary claim. Remove private customer information. Make sure the English sounds like you, the Spanish sounds natural, the photo is of your real food, and the Google listing matches the social posts.

Takeaway

ChatGPT can draft your posts in English and Spanish, but you still review every word.

En resumen: usa ChatGPT para el borrador, revisa tú cada texto, publica con fotos reales y un español natural, y no pegues datos privados de tus clientes.
